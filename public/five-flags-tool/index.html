About this tool

This is a private screener for applying Five Flags Theory to practical residency and tax planning. The original "five flags" are citizenship, legal residence, business base, asset haven, and playground — expanded here into eight measurable dimensions.

Dimensions

    Methodology

    Each country is scored 1-10 on every dimension. Scores are directional estimates based on public regulatory sources (OECD CRS participant lists, Henley Passport Index 2025/26, national tax authority publications, Nomad Capitalist rankings, CoinGecko regulatory trackers, Numbeo lifestyle indices).

    The overall score is a weighted average — adjust weights in the sidebar to match your priorities. A tax-focused flag planner might weigh Territorial/Tax Rate at 30% and Travel at 5%; a digital nomad might do the inverse.

    Important caveats

    • Regulations change frequently. Verify with a qualified advisor before taking action.
    • CRS scores reward low exposure — not useful if you're fully tax-compliant.
    • US citizens remain on citizenship-based taxation regardless of residence (FATCA applies everywhere).
    • Visa-free counts are for the country's passport, not residents-on-other-passports.
